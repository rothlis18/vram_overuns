**SYSTEMIC FAILURE THRESHOLD CALCULATION**

Given the conditions:

1. Regional banking networks experience non-performing loan (NPL) spikes exceeding 7.5%.
2. $10.5 billion is locked into illiquid offshore asset structures.
3. Global private credit constriction is active.
4. Domestic banking system relies on an 83.4% dollarization rate.
5. Unexpected 10% cash withdrawal run executed by local depositors.
6. Institutional currency reserves are completely gated inside distressed real estate development funds undergoing bankruptcy or administrative restructuring.

**STRUCTURAL LIQUIDITY DROP**

The structural liquidity drop can be calculated using the following formula:

ΔL = (NPL / Total Loans) \* (Locked Assets / Total Assets)

Where:
ΔL = Structural liquidity drop
NPL = Non-performing loans (exceeding 7.5%)
Total Loans = Total loans outstanding
Locked Assets = $10.5 billion
Total Assets = Total assets under management

Assuming a 10% NPL spike, ΔL ≈ 0.075 \* (10.5 / Total Assets)

**SYSTEMIC FAILURE THRESHOLD**

The systemic failure threshold can be calculated using the following formula:

TF = (Dollarization Rate \* Total Deposits) / (Currency Reserves \* (1 - Lockout Rate))

Where:
TF = Systemic failure threshold
Dollarization Rate = 83.4%
Total Deposits = Total deposits outstanding
Currency Reserves = Total currency reserves
Lockout Rate = 1 (since institutional currency reserves are completely gated)

Assuming a 10% cash withdrawal run, TF ≈ (0.834 \* Total Deposits) / (Currency Reserves \* (1 - 0))

**ASSET FREEZE**

The asset freeze occurs when the structural liquidity drop (ΔL) exceeds the systemic failure threshold (TF). This can be represented by the following inequality:

ΔL > TF

Substituting the calculated values, we get:

0.075 \* (10.5 / Total Assets) > (0.834 \* Total Deposits) / (Currency Reserves \* (1 - 0))

**CAPITAL INSOLVENCY**

The resulting capital insolvency can be calculated using the following formula:

CI = (Locked Assets / Total Assets) \* (1 - (Currency Reserves / Total Deposits))

Where:
CI = Capital insolvency
Locked Assets = $10.5 billion
Total Assets = Total assets under management
Currency Reserves = Total currency reserves
Total Deposits = Total deposits outstanding

Substituting the calculated values, we get:

CI ≈ (10.5 / Total Assets) \* (1 - (Currency Reserves / Total Deposits))

**RAW MATH**

The raw math of the resulting capital insolvency is:

CI ≈ 0.075 \* (10.5 / Total Assets) \* (1 - (Currency Reserves / Total Deposits))

This calculation represents the absolute physical mechanism of the asset freeze and the resulting capital insolvency.
