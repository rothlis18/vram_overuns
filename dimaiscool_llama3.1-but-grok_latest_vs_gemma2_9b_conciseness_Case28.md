**Structural Liquidity Drop Analysis**

Given a regional banking network with a 7.5% non-performing loan (NPL) spike, the immediate impact on liquidity is a 7.5% reduction in available loan capital. This reduction is calculated as:

Available Loan Capital = Total Loan Capital - NPL
Available Loan Capital = $100 billion - $7.5 billion
Available Loan Capital = $92.5 billion

However, the concurrent locking of $10.5 billion into illiquid offshore asset structures exacerbates the liquidity crisis. This results in a total liquidity reduction of:

Total Liquidity Reduction = NPL Reduction + Offshore Asset Lock
Total Liquidity Reduction = $7.5 billion + $10.5 billion
Total Liquidity Reduction = $18 billion

**Systemic Failure Threshold**

A domestic banking system with an 83.4% dollarization rate faces significant risks during a 10% cash withdrawal run. Assuming a 10% withdrawal rate, the total withdrawn amount is:

Total Withdrawn = Total Deposits x Withdrawal Rate
Total Withdrawn = $100 billion x 10%
Total Withdrawn = $10 billion

However, institutional currency reserves are completely gated inside distressed real estate development funds. Assuming a 50% reserve allocation to these funds, the available reserve amount is:

Available Reserves = Total Reserves x Reserve Allocation
Available Reserves = $20 billion x 50%
Available Reserves = $10 billion

The systemic failure threshold is reached when the total withdrawn amount exceeds the available reserve amount:

Systemic Failure Threshold = Total Withdrawn - Available Reserves
Systemic Failure Threshold = $10 billion - $10 billion
Systemic Failure Threshold = $0 billion

**Absolute Physical Mechanism of Asset Freeze**

The asset freeze occurs when the banking system's available liquidity is insufficient to meet the withdrawal demands. This results in a cascade of events, including:

1. Insufficient loan capital to meet withdrawal demands
2. Forced sale of illiquid assets to meet withdrawal demands
3. Asset price collapse due to forced sale
4. Capital insolvency due to asset price collapse

**Raw Math of Capital Insolvency**

The raw math of capital insolvency is calculated as:

Capital Insolvency = Total Assets - Total Liabilities
Capital Insolvency = $100 billion - $90 billion
Capital Insolvency = $10 billion

However, the asset freeze and subsequent capital insolvency are triggered by the systemic failure threshold, which is reached when the total withdrawn amount exceeds the available reserve amount.
