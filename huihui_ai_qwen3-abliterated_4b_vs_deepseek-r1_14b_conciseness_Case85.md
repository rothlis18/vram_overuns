Where L = total loans, D = total deposits.  
Given 83.4% dollarization, reserves (R) = 0.834D.  
Distressed real estate funds = 0.834D, locked in bankruptcy.  
Available liquidity = 0.166D (non-dollar reserves) + (L - 0.075L) = 0.166D + 0.925L.  
Required liquidity = 0.1D.  
Threshold: 0.1D > 0.166D + 0.925L → 0.1D - 0.166D > 0.925L → -0.066D > 0.925L → L < -0.0715D.
