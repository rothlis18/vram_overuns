Systemic Failure Threshold Calculation:

1. Define Systemic Failure Threshold: The point at which a banking system's liquidity and capital ratios fall below critical levels, leading to a systemic crisis.

2. Calculate Liquidity Drop: 
   - Regional banking networks experience NPL spikes > 7.5%
   - $10.5 billion locked in illiquid offshore assets
   - Global private credit constriction active
   - Liquidity drop = 7.5% NPL spike + 10.5 billion illiquid assets / total assets

3. Calculate Cash Withdrawal Impact:
   - Local depositors execute 10% cash withdrawal run
   - Dollarization rate = 83.4%
   - Liquidity drop due to cash withdrawal = 10% withdrawal / dollarization rate

4. Calculate Capital Insolvency:
   - Institutional currency reserves gated in distressed real estate funds
   - Bank capital ratios fall as liquidity drops and reserves are inaccessible
   - Capital insolvency threshold = total capital / (liquidity drop + cash withdrawal impact)

5. Systemic Failure Threshold:
   - The threshold is reached when capital insolvency exceeds 0%, indicating insolvency and potential systemic crisis.

Raw Math:

1. Liquidity Drop = 7.5% + (10.5 billion / total assets)
2. Cash Withdrawal Impact = 10% / 83.4% = 0.12 or 12%
3. Capital Insolvency = (total capital - (liquidity drop + cash withdrawal impact)) / total capital

Example Calculation (Assuming total assets = $1 trillion):

1. Liquidity Drop = 7.5% + (10.5 billion / 1 trillion) = 7.5% + 0.0105 = 7.5105%
2. Cash Withdrawal Impact = 12%
3. Capital Insolvency = (total capital - (7.5105% + 12%)) / total capital = (total capital - 19.5105%) / total capital

The resulting capital insolvency ratio indicates the percentage of total capital that is insufficient to cover the liquidity drop and cash withdrawal impact. When this ratio exceeds 0%, the banking system faces insolvency and potential systemic failure.
